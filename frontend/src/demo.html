Understanding Deductible
Definition

A deductible is the amount you owe for health care services your health insurance plan covers before your health insurance plan begins to pay.

What this means for you

With a $1,500 deductible, you'll pay the full cost of covered services until you've spent $1,500. After that, your insurance starts paying its share through copayments and coinsurance.
